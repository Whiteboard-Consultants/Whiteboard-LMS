# Quick Start: Instructor Grading

## 🎯 Get Started in 3 Steps

### Step 1: Access Your Grading Dashboard
Navigate to `/instructor/grading` in your browser

**You'll see:**
- Statistics: Pending vs Reviewed assessments
- Pending list: Assessments waiting for your review
- Already Reviewed: Completed gradings

### Step 2: Select an Assessment to Grade
Click the blue **"Grade Now"** button on any pending assessment

**You'll see:**
- Student name and submission date
- Course and assessment title
- All descriptive questions the student answered
- Student's answers in blue boxes
- Suggested model answers in green boxes

### Step 3: Provide Feedback
1. Read the student's answers
2. Compare against model answers
3. Write feedback in the text box (required)
4. Optionally enter a score (0-100)
5. Click **"Submit Feedback"**

**That's it!** You'll be redirected back to the dashboard.

---

## 📊 Understanding Your Dashboard

### Pending Review (Orange)
These assessments need your feedback NOW

### Reviewed (Blue)  
These are already graded. Click "View Feedback" to see what you wrote

### Statistics
- **Pending Review**: Count of assessments waiting
- **Reviewed**: Count you've already graded
- **Total Attempts**: All assessments in your courses

## ✍️ Writing Good Feedback

### Do's ✅
- Be specific: "Your explanation of X is missing step 3"
- Give examples: "Consider approaching this like..."
- Acknowledge effort: "Good attempt, but..."
- Suggest improvement: "Next time, try..."
- Be encouraging: Students worked hard!

### Don'ts ❌
- Don't just say "wrong"
- Don't be harsh or discouraging
- Don't skip explaining your reasoning
- Don't provide the complete answer (let them learn)

### Feedback Template
```
Strengths:
- What they did well...

Areas for Improvement:
- Specific points to work on...

Next Steps:
- How to approach this differently...
```

---

## 🎯 Scoring (Optional)

The score field is completely optional. Use it when you want to:
- Give a numerical grade (0-100)
- Track performance metrics
- Weigh descriptive answers toward course grade

Leave it blank to provide **feedback only** (recommended for most cases).

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Grade in batches**: Set aside time weekly to grade pending assessments
2. **Take notes**: Write feedback right away while you remember why they lost points
3. **Be consistent**: Similar answers should get similar feedback
4. **Reference standards**: Point to course learning objectives
5. **Encourage retry**: Suggest when students should attempt again

---

## ❓ Common Questions

**Q: How long should feedback be?**  
A: 2-4 sentences minimum. More detail = better learning.

**Q: Should I always give a score?**  
A: Only if required by your course grading policy. Feedback alone is valuable.

**Q: Can I edit feedback after submitting?**  
A: You can submit new feedback, but the student sees the latest version.

**Q: What if I make a typo?**  
A: Re-grade the assessment with corrected feedback. This overwrites the previous version.

**Q: Do students see my feedback immediately?**  
A: Yes, immediately after you submit.
