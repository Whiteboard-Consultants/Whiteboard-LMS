<h2>What is a Good GMAT Score?</h2>

<p>The <strong>GMAT (Graduate Management Admission Test)</strong> is standardized test required for MBA program admissions worldwide. Your GMAT score is ONE of the most important factors in MBA applications (along with GPA, work experience, and essays).</p>

<p><strong>GMAT Score Range:</strong> 200-800 points</p>

<p><strong>What's Considered "Good"?</strong></p>
<ul>
  <li>600-640: Above Average (60th-75th percentile)</li>
  <li>650-700: Strong (75th-88th percentile)</li>
  <li>700-750: Very Strong (88th-97th percentile) - Top 25 MBA Programs</li>
  <li>750+: Exceptional (97th-99th percentile) - Top 10 MBA Programs</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Average GMAT Scores by Program Tier (2024-2025):</strong></p>
<ul>
  <li>🏆 <strong>Top 10 MBA:</strong> 710-730 average</li>
  <li>🥈 <strong>Top 25 MBA:</strong> 680-710 average</li>
  <li>🥉 <strong>Top 50 MBA:</strong> 650-690 average</li>
  <li>📊 <strong>Top 100 MBA:</strong> 600-650 average</li>
  <li>📈 <strong>Online/Executive MBA:</strong> 550-600 average</li>
</ul>

<h2>How is GMAT Scored? Section Breakdown</h2>

<p>The GMAT has 4 sections:</p>

<h3>1. Analytical Writing Assessment (AWA)</h3>
<ul>
  <li>⏱️ Time: 30 minutes</li>
  <li>🎯 Task: Write essay analyzing an argument</li>
  <li>📊 Score: 0-6 (not included in 200-800 total)</li>
  <li>💡 Importance: Rarely affects admissions; shows writing ability</li>
</ul>

<h3>2. Integrated Reasoning (IR)</h3>
<ul>
  <li>⏱️ Time: 30 minutes</li>
  <li>🎯 Task: Analyze data tables, graphs, interpret information</li>
  <li>📊 Score: 1-8 (not included in 200-800 total)</li>
  <li>💡 Importance: Growing importance; shows data interpretation</li>
</ul>

<h3>3. Quantitative Reasoning (Quant)</h3>
<ul>
  <li>⏱️ Time: 62 minutes, 31 questions</li>
  <li>🎯 Task: Data Sufficiency, Problem Solving (math)</li>
  <li>📊 Score: 6-51 (converted to 200-800 scale)</li>
  <li>💡 Importance: CRITICAL for GMAT - tests problem-solving</li>
  <li>Topics: Algebra, geometry, arithmetic, statistics, probability</li>
</ul>

<h3>4. Verbal Reasoning</h3>
<ul>
  <li>⏱️ Time: 65 minutes, 36 questions</li>
  <li>🎯 Task: Reading Comprehension, Critical Reasoning, Sentence Correction</li>
  <li>📊 Score: 6-51 (converted to 200-800 scale)</li>
  <li>💡 Importance: CRITICAL for GMAT - tests reasoning & English</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Total GMAT Score (200-800) = Combination of Quant + Verbal</strong></p>
<ul>
  <li>Both Quant and Verbal equally weighted</li>
  <li>Typical breakdown: Quant 50th percentile + Verbal 50th percentile = ~600 score</li>
  <li>High Quant (90th) + High Verbal (90th) = ~720 score</li>
</ul>

<h2>GMAT Score Timeline & Retesting</h2>

<h3>How Long is GMAT Score Valid?</h3>
<ul>
  <li>✅ GMAT scores valid for 5 years</li>
  <li>✅ Can submit scores to MBA programs anytime within 5 years</li>
  <li>✅ Most universities accept scores up to 3 years old</li>
</ul>

<h3>GMAT Retesting Policy</h3>
<ul>
  <li>✅ Can retake GMAT after 16 calendar days</li>
  <li>✅ Can take GMAT maximum 5 times per year</li>
  <li>✅ Can take GMAT maximum 8 times lifetime</li>
  <li>✅ All scores visible to universities (they see all attempts)</li>
  <li>✅ Most universities take "highest" score, some take "average"</li>
</ul>

<h3>Realistic Score Improvement</h3>
<ul>
  <li>First attempt: Average improvement needed from baseline</li>
  <li>Second attempt: 20-30 point improvement (with focused prep)</li>
  <li>Third attempt: 10-15 point improvement (diminishing returns)</li>
  <li>Fourth+ attempts: 5-10 point improvement (study less effective)</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Pro Tip:</strong> Most successful MBA applicants score within 20 points of target on first or second attempt. If you're not improving after 2-3 attempts, likely need to change study strategy or accept current score.</p>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions About GMAT Scores</h2>

<div style="background-color: #f0f9ff; padding: 20px; border-left: 4px solid #0284c7; margin: 20px 0; border-radius: 4px;">

<h4>Q: What is a competitive GMAT score for top MBA programs?</h4>
<p><strong>A:</strong> 700-750 is competitive for top 25 MBA programs. Top 10 programs (Harvard, Stanford, Wharton) average 730+. Score above 88th percentile (700+) makes you competitive for most prestigious MBA programs.</p>

<h4>Q: Can I get into MBA with 600 GMAT?</h4>
<p><strong>A:</strong> Yes, but strategy matters. 600 GMAT: Top 100 MBA programs possible with strong profile (work experience, leadership, essays). Less likely for top 25 programs unless other factors exceptionally strong (CEO experience, unique background).</p>

<h4>Q: Is GMAT harder than IELTS?</h4>
<p><strong>A:</strong> Different difficulties. IELTS tests English fluency (requires 6+ months preparation). GMAT tests business reasoning and quant (requires 2-4 months). Both tough but different skills tested. Indians often find GMAT harder due to quant complexity.</p>

<h4>Q: How many times should I retake GMAT?</h4>
<p><strong>A:</strong> Ideal: 1-2 attempts. If first attempt: 600-640, take once more (improved prep). If second attempt: still 600-640, consider accepting or changing study method. Most admissions officers see many attempts negatively (suggests lack of mastery).</p>

<h4>Q: Can I use GMAT score for 2-3 years later?</h4>
<p><strong>A:</strong> Yes! GMAT valid for 5 years. Most universities accept scores up to 3 years old. Plan: Take GMAT 6-12 months before MBA application. Don't retake if score already strong.</p>

<h4>Q: Do all MBA programs require GMAT?</h4>
<p><strong>A:</strong> Most top 50 MBA programs require GMAT (or GRE). Some mid-tier and online MBA programs now have "GMAT Optional" policy. But GMAT submission strongly recommended if score is competitive (700+).</p>
