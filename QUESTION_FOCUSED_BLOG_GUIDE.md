## 🎯 Overview & Strategy

### Why Question-Focused Content?
- **Google Trend:** 72% of searches are now question-based
- **Featured Snippets:** 40%+ of questions get featured snippet treatment
- **Traffic Impact:** Question-based queries typically have higher conversion rates
- **AEO Optimization:** Answer Engine Optimization targets both Google and AI tools
- **PAA Visibility:** "People Also Ask" sections can drive 20-30% additional CTR

### Content Philosophy
Each blog post should:
1. **Answer a specific question** users are searching for
2. **Provide comprehensive information** (not just a quick answer)
3. **Include related FAQs** showing depth of knowledge
4. **Have comparison tables** where applicable (featured snippet format)
5. **Include actionable steps** (listicles, guides, tutorials)
6. **Link internally** to relevant courses and services
7. **End with CTA** (schedule consultation, view course)

## 📝 Content Writing Guidelines

### Tone & Style
- **Professional but conversational** - Write like you're explaining to a friend
- **Action-oriented** - Use imperative mood ("Do X", "Avoid Y")
- **Data-backed** - Support claims with statistics and examples
- **Scannable** - Use short paragraphs, bullet points, bold text
- **Empathetic** - Address user pain points and concerns

### Structure Template (For Each Post)

```markdown
# [Question Title]

## Introduction (100-150 words)
- Hook: Start with a compelling statement or question
- Context: Why is this question important?
- Benefit: What will reader learn?
- Word count: Keep it snappy

## Quick Answer Section (50-100 words)
- Direct answer to the main question
- Brief comparison (if applicable)
- Point to detailed sections below

## Main Content Sections (2,000-2,500 words)
### Section 1: [First aspect]
### Section 2: [Second aspect]
### Section 3: [Third aspect]
### Comparison Table (if applicable)
- Always use featured snippet format
- Make it scannable and comparison-focused

## FAQ Section (500-800 words)
- 8-10 related questions
- Concise answers (50-150 words each)
- Link to main content where relevant

## Internal Links (2-4)
- Link to related courses
- Link to related blog posts
- Link to service pages
- Natural placement, not forced

## Call-to-Action (50-100 words)
- Relevant to the post topic
- Link to course, contact form, or destination guide
- Make it a natural next step

## Conclusion (100-150 words)
- Summarize key points
- Reinforce main message
- Soft CTA
```

### SEO Best Practices in Content
1. **Include primary keyword** in:
   - Title (first 60 characters)
   - Meta description
   - First 100 words of content
   - At least one heading (H2)
   - Throughout content naturally (2-3% keyword density)

2. **Include related keywords** naturally:
   - Variations of main keyword
   - Long-tail versions
   - Question formats ("how to", "what is", "where to")

3. **Format for Featured Snippets:**
   - Lists (numbered or bullet)
   - Tables (comparison, specs, scores)
   - Definitions (concise explanations)
   - Steps (how-to guides)

4. **Optimize for readability:**
   - Use short paragraphs (2-3 sentences)
   - Bold important terms
   - Use subheadings (H2, H3)
   - Add visuals/tables
   - Keep line length under 100 characters
