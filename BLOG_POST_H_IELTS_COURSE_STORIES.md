<h2>What's Inside an 8-Week IELTS Intensive Course?</h2>

<p>You've decided to take IELTS seriously. Good decision. But what exactly is an "intensive course"? How does it work? And most importantly—will YOU see real results?</p>

<p>This article breaks down exactly what happens week-by-week in our 8-week IELTS intensive program, and shows real student transformations to prove it works.</p>

<h2>The IELTS Challenge (Why You Need a Course)</h2>

<p>IELTS is NOT just vocabulary. It's:</p>
<ul>
  <li>🎯 <strong>Listening:</strong> Understanding 4 different accents at native speed (first time, no repeats)</li>
  <li>📖 <strong>Reading:</strong> 3 passages, 40 questions, 60 minutes. Need to read 220+ words/minute with comprehension</li>
  <li>✍️ <strong>Writing:</strong> 2 different task types, each graded on 4 criteria (Task achievement, coherence, grammar, vocabulary)</li>
  <li>💬 <strong>Speaking:</strong> 12-15 minute conversation with human examiner. Graded on fluency, pronunciation, grammar, vocabulary</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Many students get stuck at 5.5-6.0 bands because:</strong></p>
<ul>
  <li>Don't understand what each band descriptor actually means</li>
  <li>Can't identify common mistake patterns (keep repeating errors)</li>
  <li>Lack time management skills for Reading (run out of time)</li>
  <li>Get no feedback on Speaking (can't improve pronunciation/grammar alone)</li>
  <li>Write essays without knowing band requirements</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>A structured course teaches you exactly what examiners are listening for.</strong></p>

<h2>8-Week IELTS Intensive Course: Week-by-Week Breakdown</h2>

<h3>WEEK 1: Foundation & Diagnostic Testing</h3>

<p><strong>Focus:</strong> Understand IELTS format, identify your current level, create personalized roadmap</p>

<p><strong>What Happens:</strong></p>
<ul>
  <li>✅ <strong>Live Kickoff Session (2 hours):</strong> Meet instructors, understand course structure, ask questions</li>
  <li>✅ <strong>Full Diagnostic IELTS Mock Test (3 hours):</strong> Under real exam conditions (you at home, timer running)</li>
  <li>✅ <strong>Expert Score Analysis (1.5 hours):</strong> Detailed feedback on EVERY section</li>
  <li>✅ <strong>Personalized Study Plan (30 min):</strong> Based on your weak areas (not generic)</li>
  <li>✅ <strong>Module 1 Learning:</strong> IELTS Scoring System (understand bands 1-9, what examiners look for)</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Time Commitment:</strong> 6-8 hours total</p>

<p><strong>Key Takeaway:</strong> Know EXACTLY where you stand and what you need to improve</p>

<h3>WEEK 2: Listening Foundation + Reading Speed Training</h3>

<p><strong>Focus:</strong> Listening accuracy + Reading speed (problem for many)</p>

<p><strong>What Happens:</strong></p>

<p><strong>Listening (3 hours):</strong></p>
<ul>
  <li>Learn all question types (multiple choice, matching, flow chart, diagram labeling, form completion, short answer)</li>
  <li>Understand listening traps (what makes answers wrong)</li>
  <li>Pronunciation training (British, American, Australian accents)</li>
  <li>Note-taking strategies (what to write during listening)</li>
  <li>Assignment: 2 listening practice tests with detailed feedback</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Reading (3 hours):</strong></p>
<ul>
  <li>Speed reading techniques (skim, scan, detailed read)</li>
  <li>Vocabulary in context (don't need to know every word)</li>
  <li>Time management (allocate time per passage wisely)</li>
  <li>Practice: 1 full reading test timed</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Time Commitment:</strong> 6 hours class + 5 hours homework = 11 hours</p>

<p><strong>Key Takeaway:</strong> Read faster (200+ wpm) without losing accuracy</p>

<h3>WEEK 3: Writing Task 1 + Writing Task 2 Introduction</h3>

<p><strong>Focus:</strong> Master letter writing (Task 1) and essay structure (Task 2)</p>

<p><strong>Writing Task 1 (2.5 hours):</strong></p>
<ul>
  <li>Understand 3 letter types: Formal, Semi-formal, Informal</li>
  <li>Letter structure (salutation, opening, body, closing)</li>
  <li>Common mistakes (tone, clarity, organization)</li>
  <li>Assignment: Write 2 letters, get expert feedback</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Writing Task 2 (2.5 hours):</strong></p>
<ul>
  <li>Understand 5 essay types: Opinion, Discuss both sides, Problem-solution, Advantages-disadvantages, Direct question</li>
  <li>Essay structure: Introduction (hook + thesis), 2-3 body paragraphs, conclusion</li>
  <li>Vocabulary for essays (transition words, academic phrases)</li>
  <li>Assignment: Write 1 timed essay (20 min planning + writing)</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Time Commitment:</strong> 5 hours class + 4 hours homework = 9 hours</p>

<p><strong>Key Takeaway:</strong> Know exactly what examiners want to see in writing</p>

<h3>WEEK 4: Speaking Part 1, 2, 3 + Intensive Speaking Practice</h3>

<p><strong>Focus:</strong> Overcome speaking anxiety, practice with feedback</p>

<p><strong>Speaking Structure (2 hours):</strong></p>
<ul>
  <li>Part 1: Introduction questions (4-5 minutes of casual conversation)</li>
  <li>Part 2: Long turn (speak 1-2 minutes on given topic, using cue card)</li>
  <li>Part 3: Follow-up discussion (abstract/complex questions)</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Intensive Practice (4 hours over week):</strong></p>
<ul>
  <li>3 mock speaking tests (1-on-1 with instructor, recorded)</li>
  <li>Pronunciation analysis and correction</li>
  <li>Fluency drills (speak without pausing/hesitation)</li>
  <li>Grammar correction (fix common spoken errors)</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Time Commitment:</strong> 2 hours class + 6 hours homework/practice = 8 hours</p>

<p><strong>Key Takeaway:</strong> Confidence in speaking + feedback on actual mistakes</p>

<h3>WEEK 5: Advanced Reading Strategies + Writing Refinement</h3>

<p><strong>Focus:</strong> Master difficult reading passages + improve writing band scores</p>

<p><strong>Reading (2.5 hours):</strong></p>
<ul>
  <li>Advanced passage types (academic topics: science, history, social sciences)</li>
  <li>Complex question types (identify multiple answers, true/false/not given)</li>
  <li>Time management in real test (leave no question blank)</li>
  <li>Practice: 1 full reading test with strategy debrief</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Writing (2.5 hours):</strong></p>
<ul>
  <li>Band 8 writing characteristics (what makes an essay 8 vs 6?)</li>
  <li>Vocabulary variety (synonyms, less common words, collocation)</li>
  <li>Grammar complexity (complex sentences, varied structures)</li>
  <li>Task achievement focus (fully address the prompt)</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Assignments:</strong></p>
<ul>
  <li>Write 3 Task 1 letters (get detailed feedback on each)</li>
  <li>Write 2 Task 2 essays (scored on band descriptors)</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Time Commitment:</strong> 5 hours class + 6 hours homework = 11 hours</p>

<p><strong>Key Takeaway:</strong> Understand what separates band 6 from band 8 in writing</p>

<h3>WEEK 6: Listening Accuracy + Speaking Fluency Intensive</h3>

<p><strong>Focus:</strong> Fix common mistakes, build speed, perfect accuracy</p>

<p><strong>Listening (3 hours):</strong></p>
<ul>
  <li>Advanced listening passages (abstract topics, technical vocabulary)</li>
  <li>Common spelling mistakes (fix them now)</li>
  <li>Accent challenges (practice Australian + Singaporean accents)</li>
  <li>Practice: 2 full listening tests timed, scored, analyzed</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Speaking Intensive (3 hours):</strong></p>
<ul>
  <li>5 mock speaking tests (recorded, graded on band descriptors)</li>
  <li>Personalized error correction (what YOU specifically struggle with)</li>
  <li>Part 2 practice (how to speak 2 minutes without stopping)</li>
  <li>Part 3 practice (how to handle difficult abstract questions)</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Time Commitment:</strong> 6 hours class + 8 hours practice = 14 hours</p>

<p><strong>Key Takeaway:</strong> Reach consistent band 7+ on practice tests</p>

<h3>WEEK 7: Full Practice Tests + Individual Consultations</h3>

<p><strong>Focus:</strong> Simulate real exam, identify last-minute improvements</p>

<p><strong>What Happens:</strong></p>
<ul>
  <li>✅ <strong>Full Practice Test #1 (3 hours):</strong> Under real exam conditions. Timed, scored, analyzed.</li>
  <li>✅ <strong>Full Practice Test #2 (3 hours):</strong> 2-3 days later. Compare score progression.</li>
  <li>✅ <strong>1-on-1 Consultation (30 min per student):</strong> Instructor reviews your practice tests, identifies final weak areas, gives specific advice</li>
  <li>✅ <strong>Targeted Drills (2-3 hours):</strong> Focus on your specific weak areas (e.g., if you're weak in Reading, do targeted reading drills)</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Time Commitment:</strong> 6 hours class + 6-8 hours homework = 12-14 hours</p>

<p><strong>Key Takeaway:</strong> Know your real test-day score prediction (typically accurate within 0.5 bands)</p>

<h3>WEEK 8: Final Preparation + Test Day Strategy</h3>

<p><strong>Focus:</strong> Build confidence, remove anxiety, go into exam ready</p>

<p><strong>What Happens:</strong></p>
<ul>
  <li>✅ <strong>Final Practice Test (3 hours):</strong> Last chance to practice under real conditions</li>
  <li>✅ <strong>Test Day Strategy Session (1.5 hours):</strong> What to do on exam day, how to manage time, what to bring</li>
  <li>✅ <strong>Anxiety Management & Confidence Building (1 hour):</strong> Mental preparation</li>
  <li>✅ <strong>Q&A Session (1 hour):</strong> Answer any final questions</li>
  <li>✅ <strong>Milestone Celebration:</strong> You've completed 8 weeks of intense prep!</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Time Commitment:</strong> 5-6 hours class + light review = 6-8 hours</p>
